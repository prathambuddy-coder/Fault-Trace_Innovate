🚆 Real-World Use Case: Metro Air Compressors

In modern metro trains, the Compressed Air Unit (CAU) powers pneumatic brakes, train doors, and suspension. If a purge valve sticks open, the train loses pressure and halts, stranding thousands of commuters. FaultTrace turns complex sensor signals into an instant, human-readable troubleshooting sheet.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Field Checklist For Technician

1. Depressurize unit & apply electrical Lock-Out / Tag-Out (LOTO).
2. Inspect dryer silencer for audible hissing while compressor is loaded.
3. Connect test gauge to port M1 (< 0.05 bar backpressure required).
4. Disassemble valve body; inspect brass seat for grit or particulate sludge.
5. Reassemble with new Viton seal kit and torque bonnet bolts to 18 Nm.
